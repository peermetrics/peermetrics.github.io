How it works

Instrument, monitor, troubleshoot

Peermetrics gives teams a clean workflow from stats collection to issue investigation.
Step 1

Connect your app

Add the SDK or use @peermetrics/webrtc-stats to capture WebRTC telemetry from your real-time application.

Step 2

Monitor every session

Track call quality indicators, browser and device context, and the events that explain why user experience changed.

Step 3

Resolve issues faster

Move from a vague complaint to specific troubleshooting data around packet loss, jitter, connection states, and peer behavior.

What you can monitor

Monitor the metrics that break real calls

Peermetrics is designed for teams diagnosing live audio and video issues, not just collecting generic analytics.

  • Packet loss, jitter, bitrate, and round-trip time
  • Connection failures, device context, and browser behavior
  • Session-level debugging for support, engineering, and operations
  • Analytics across self-hosted deployments and custom integrations
Integrations
